# NovaMart Exchange Policy

> **Fictional Notice:** This is a fictional document created solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes for the NovaMart Assistant project. It does not represent any real company or legal policy.

## 1. Exchange Eligibility
Items eligible for return can also be exchanged within 30 calendar days of purchase. Exchanges can be processed at any NovaMart retail store or coordinated through online customer support.

## 2. Even Exchanges
An even exchange is permitted when swapping the exact same item for a different size or color, provided the original retail price has not changed.

## 3. Price Differences
If exchanging for a product of higher value, the customer pays the remaining difference. If exchanging for a product of lower value, the remaining balance is refunded to the original payment method or issued as store credit.

## 4. Defective or Damaged Replacements
If an item arrives damaged or is found defective within 14 days of receipt, NovaMart provides an immediate replacement with free expedited shipping and no restocking fees.
