# NovaMart Customer Support and Escalations

> **Fictional Notice:** This is a fictional document created solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes for the NovaMart Assistant project. It does not represent any real company or legal policy.

## 1. Support Channels and Operating Hours
NovaMart customer support is accessible through three official communication channels:
- **Live Chat:** Available 24/7 on the NovaMart mobile app and website.
- **Telephone Support:** Toll-free 1-800-NOVAMART, Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM EST.
- **Email Support:** `support@novamart-example.com` with a guaranteed initial response within 24 hours.

## 2. Store-Level Escalation
For urgent in-store issues that store associates cannot resolve, associates must escalate the case directly to the Floor Lead or Duty Store Manager on shift.

## 3. Executive Inquiries and Formal Complaints
Formal disputes unresolved after 5 business days are forwarded to the NovaMart Customer Relations Ombudsman with a dedicated case resolution tracking ID.
