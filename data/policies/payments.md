# NovaMart Payment Methods Policy

> **Fictional Notice:** This is a fictional document created solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes for the NovaMart Assistant project. It does not represent any real company or legal policy.

## 1. Accepted Payment Forms
NovaMart accepts the following payment methods across all stores and online checkouts:
- Major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover).
- Contactless mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay).
- NovaMart Gift Cards and Store Credit vouchers.
- Cash payments (in physical retail stores only).

## 2. Installment Plans
Orders totaling $100 or more qualify for NovaMart FlexPay, allowing customers to split their purchase into 4 interest-free bi-weekly installments via verified third-party partners.

## 3. Currency and Billing Security
All transactions are processed in USD. NovaMart employs end-to-end PCI-DSS compliant tokenization; full card numbers are never stored in internal retail databases.
