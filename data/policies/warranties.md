# NovaMart Warranty and Repairs Policy

> **Fictional Notice:** This is a fictional document created solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes for the NovaMart Assistant project. It does not represent any real company or legal policy.

## 1. Manufacturer Limited Warranty
All electronic devices and home appliances sold through NovaMart include the standard original manufacturer warranty, typically valid for 1 year from the purchase date for defects in materials or workmanship.

## 2. NovaMart CarePlus Protection
Customers can purchase NovaMart CarePlus within 30 days of hardware purchase. CarePlus extends coverage up to 3 years and includes accidental liquid damage, mechanical failure, and battery degradation below 80%.

## 3. Warranty Exclusions
Standard warranties do not cover cosmetic wear, intentional abuse, unauthorized third-party repairs, or losses caused by natural disasters.

## 4. Submitting a Warranty Claim
To file a repair or replacement claim under CarePlus, customers present their device and CarePlus registration number at any NovaMart Tech Desk or submit an online claim portal ticket.
