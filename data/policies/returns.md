# NovaMart Return Policy

> **Fictional Notice:** This is a fictional document created solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes for the NovaMart Assistant project. It does not represent any real company or legal policy.

## 1. Return Window
Customers may return eligible items within 30 calendar days from the date of purchase or delivery. Proof of purchase (physical receipt, digital order confirmation, or NovaMart rewards account lookup) is required for all returns.

## 2. Item Condition Requirements
To qualify for a full refund, items must be in new, unused condition, with all original tags, manuals, and protective packaging intact. 

## 3. Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned once opened:
- Intimate apparel and personal hygiene products.
- Digital software licenses, downloadable content, and prepaid gift cards.
- Clearance items clearly marked as "Final Sale".

## 4. Refund Processing
Approved returns are refunded to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after inspection. If the original payment method is unavailable, store credit will be issued.
