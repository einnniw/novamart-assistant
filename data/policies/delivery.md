# NovaMart Delivery and Shipping Policy

> **Fictional Notice:** This is a fictional document created solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes for the NovaMart Assistant project. It does not represent any real company or legal policy.

## 1. Shipping Options and Timelines
NovaMart offers three shipping tiers for domestic online purchases:
- **Standard Shipping:** Delivers within 3 to 5 business days. Free on orders over $50; otherwise, a flat fee of $4.99 applies.
- **Express Shipping:** Delivers within 2 business days for a flat rate of $12.99.
- **Same-Day Store Delivery:** Available in select metropolitan zones for orders placed before 1:00 PM local time ($9.99 flat fee).

## 2. Order Tracking
Once an order ships from a NovaMart distribution hub, an automated tracking number is generated and sent via email and SMS. Tracking updates appear within 24 hours of package dispatch.

## 3. Delivery Restrictions
We currently do not ship to international destinations, P.O. boxes for oversized freight, or APO/FPO addresses. Signature on delivery is mandatory for electronics and orders valued over $500.
